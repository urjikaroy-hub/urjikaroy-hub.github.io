A C++ Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Base checking engine designed to analyze musical notation based on AP Music Theory Rules. This is my passion project for senior year and evaluates 4-part harmonies (SATB) against 18th century voice-leading rules to partly automate AP Music Theory grading.
